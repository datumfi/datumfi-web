The Invisible Architecture

What makes this different isn't what you see. It's what you don't have to.

TAX ENGINE
41-Year Sequencing
We model exact withdrawal order and bracket fills across all 41 years in every simulation.
What this means for you
Most retirement calculators apply a flat "tax rate" and move on. We sequence every withdrawal across 41 years — Traditional 457(b) first to fill the standard deduction and low brackets, then Traditional 401K via Rule of 55, then Roth for the rest — minimizing lifetime tax drag to the dollar. For your plan specifically, Traditional accounts deplete around Year 4 of retirement, after which every withdrawal is tax-free.
What Studio reveals
Your year-by-year federal tax bill, effective tax rate per year, standard deduction headroom available for Roth conversions, and the exact crossover year where your plan becomes 100% tax-free.
DYNAMIC BEHAVIOR
Spending Guardrails
The engine automatically cuts spending during crashes and raises it during booms to protect the portfolio.
What this means for you
Your Range isn't a one-time number — it's a living boundary. When markets drop hard, the engine automatically reduces spending to preserve the architecture. When markets boom, it gives you permission to spend more. This is how real retirees survive bad decades without running out of money. Static "4% rules" can't do this.
What Studio reveals
The exact spending cuts triggered in each stress scenario (1929 Crash, 1970s Stagflation, Japanese Lost Decade), plus the upside adjustments during favorable regimes — so you know in advance what flexibility your plan requires.
PORTFOLIO MATH
Allocation Glide-Path
We recompute volatility automatically as you de-risk from equities over time.
What this means for you
Your portfolio today is heavily weighted to equity. At age 75, it probably shouldn't be. Most retirement tools lock volatility at one number and pretend it never changes. We recompute your expected volatility every year as you de-risk — because a 60/40 portfolio in 2050 behaves mathematically differently than an 80/20 portfolio in 2030.
What Studio reveals
Your full glide-path from today through age 90, the volatility at each age, and how your Range tightens or widens as the portfolio shifts — so your architecture stays honest as your life changes.
CASHFLOW TIMELINE
Income Layering
Income has seasons. We map every one: from bridge-years, to your pension's real decay.
What this means for you
Retirement income has seasons. Bridge years (no Social Security yet, no Medicare yet) are the hardest. Then pension begins. Then Social Security layers in. Then pension loses real purchasing power to inflation. We map every transition — so your plan knows which years are heavy-lift and which are coasting.
What Studio reveals
Your exact income layering map from retirement through age 90 — including pension real decay, Social Security start-age optimization, and the specific years your portfolio bears the heaviest weight.
VALUATION INTELLIGENCE
CAPE-Dynamic Weights
When markets are expensive, we automatically shift trust toward conservative models.
What this means for you
When the market is expensive (high CAPE), historical averages lie about the future. Most retirement tools blend their models with equal weights forever. We don't. When CAPE is high, we automatically shift trust toward conservative models — because that's when the optimistic ones are most likely wrong. When CAPE is low, we shift back. Your Range reflects the market you're retiring into, not the market your grandfather retired from.
What Studio reveals
Your current CAPE regime, the exact weight shift applied to each of the four engines, and how your Range would change under a normal CAPE environment.
COST DRAG
Fee-Aware Returns
Every basis point has a name. We compute the exact drag of your holdings.
What this means for you
Every basis point compounds. A 0.5% expense ratio on a $1.3M portfolio is roughly $6,750 per year — the cost of a family vacation every year for 40 years. Most tools ignore fees. We compute the exact drag of your specific holdings across every account, every fund, every year — and build it into your Range.
What Studio reveals
Your total annual fees across all accounts, the highest-cost funds flagged for review, and the lifetime fee drag your architecture will absorb versus a low-cost alternative.

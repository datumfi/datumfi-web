=== CORP title hover ===
 Details ⓘ What it is A taxable brokerage owned by a business entity — an LLC, an S-corp, a partnership — rather than by you personally. How it works here Under the hood it’s the same open-market brokerage as your Living Room: the same holdings, the same embedded gains, the same four signal boxes and Datum Intelligence read. Every column tooltip you hover behaves exactly as it does in a personal taxable account. What’s different The account belongs to the entity, not to you. That changes who files the tax and who the creditors can reach — not how the investments themselves are taxed as they sit. We flag it as entity-owned so your net worth stays honest about what’s held personally versus through a business. What we don’t do we don’t model your entity’s tax return or its liability shield — those live with your CPA and attorney, not in this room. 

=== OTHER TAXABLE title hover ===
 Details ⓘ What it is The catch-all for any taxable account that doesn’t match a named brokerage — an odd account, a one-off, a broker we don’t have a dedicated room for yet. How it works here It runs on the exact same Living Room engine — open-market holdings, real cost basis, capital-gains treatment, the full Datum Intelligence read. Nothing is watered down; it’s a full taxable room wearing a generic label. Why it exists So a taxable account you hold never gets silently dropped just because it doesn’t have a branded home. It lands somewhere real, gets read like any other brokerage, and counts. What we don’t do we don’t guess at a category you didn’t give us — if this account is really a retirement or education wrapper, it belongs in one of those rooms instead, and you can move it any time. 

=== OTHER ASSETS title hover (R396) ===
 Details ⓘ What it is Something you own that has real worth but no ticker and no holdings to read — a private business interest, a stake in a partnership, an asset that lives outside the market. How it works here You give it a value and a note; that’s the whole room. There’s no Interior Decorating, no holdings table, no beta or yield — because there are no market positions to measure. It behaves like your Real Estate and Collectibles rooms: a single number that stands on its own. What it does It counts toward your net worth and your gross estate — it’s real value, and the estate view treats it as such. What it does not do it doesn’t join the equity-and-bond Shape, and the "Reveal" investable check steps right past it. That’s deliberate — it’s illiquid "other," not money you can rebalance or draw from like a brokerage. Walling it out keeps your investable mix honest.

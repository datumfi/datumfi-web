Privacy Choices

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of their personal information.

DATUM FI does not sell or share your personal information. We do not use advertising cookies, behavioral trackers, or third-party data brokers. Your Sketch and Studio inputs never leave your browser session. Google Fonts may log your IP address when loading fonts from their CDN — see our Privacy Policy for details.

What We Store

  • A session token in localStorage to maintain session continuity during your visit.
  • Your privacy consent preference (datum_privacy_ok) in localStorage.
  • Sketch and Studio inputs in sessionStorage — cleared when you close the browser tab.

None of this data is transmitted to any third party for advertising or analytics purposes.

Clear Your Local Data

You can delete all locally stored data from this browser at any time.
